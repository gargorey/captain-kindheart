# 5-minute demo for parents and stakeholders

**Setup:** fresh install, phone volume medium, Arabic TTS voice installed (Android: Google TTS Arabic; iOS: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Arabic).

1. **Why this exists (30s).** "Many of us have said 'the police will take you.' Captain Kindheart replaces fear with a friendly helper and celebration."
2. **Onboarding (45s).** Swipe through: meet Captain Sami ("شخصية خيالية"), the safety promises for parents, Arabic language. Type a name, for example "سارة", or show that skipping is one tap.
3. **Gentle reminder (60s).** Home → تذكير لطيف → وقت الترتيب. Sami greets "مرحبًا يا سارة!", gives one instruction, offers a choice (المكعبات / السيارات), then reassures. Tap **إيقاف** mid-sentence to show audio stops instantly.
4. **Pretend call (60s).** Home → مكالمة تخيّلية. Point out the parent explanation ("ليس مكالمة حقيقية من الشرطة"). Pick "كلمات لطيفة", start. Show the cartoon call card and the "pretend" label. Answer, let one line play, tap mute, then **إنهاء**: everything stops at once.
5. **Celebrate (60s).** Home → شجّع واحتفل → المشاركة + رقصة كابتن سامي → لنحتفل! Confetti, Sami dances, a new badge and a flower.
6. **Garden (20s).** Show flowers and badges. "No scores, no punishments, no streaks to lose."
7. **Parent controls (45s).** Settings gear → parent gate question → change the name, turn on quiet mode, edit a situation's wording using {الاسم}. Open privacy info: everything stays on the phone.

**Close:** "Emotional safety first, the parent always in control, and every good choice noticed."
